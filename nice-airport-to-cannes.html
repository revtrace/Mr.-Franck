Cannes is the easiest of the major runs from Nice Airport — right up until May, when the Film Festival closes half the Croisette and the traffic on the Bord de Mer stops moving entirely.

The rest of the year it is a straightforward thirty-something minutes. Franck's value is knowing which of those two situations you are actually in, and telling you before you build a schedule around the wrong one.

What the journey looks like

  • Met inside the arrivals hall with your name on a sign.
  • Flight tracked, so a delay costs nothing.
  • The A8 when the coast road is busy, the Bord de Mer when it isn't and you'd rather look at the sea.
  • Dropped at the door — the Martinez, the Carlton, the Majestic, the Palais des Festivals, the old port, or a villa above the town.
  • Help with the bags, which matters more than it sounds on the Croisette in August.

How else could you do it?

Honestly, including where the alternatives are the better call.

Taxi from the rank

Metered, plus supplements. Expect to queue, and expect the fare to be a surprise.

Train

There is a direct rail line along the coast, but not from the airport — you need to get into Nice-Ville or Cannes' own station first, with your luggage, and then out the other end.

Airport bus

A direct coach runs between the airport and Cannes. Reasonable value, fixed stops, limited luggage, and no help at either end.

Mr. Franck

One fixed price, agreed before you travel and including tolls and parking. Met inside arrivals, driven to the door, per car rather than per person.
