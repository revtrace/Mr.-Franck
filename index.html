Why people book a driver here

The Riviera is beautiful. Driving it is not.

Blind hairpins on the Moyenne Corniche. A 40-minute crawl into Monaco on a race weekend. Parking in Cannes in August. Roundabouts with no lane markings and a rental car you're afraid to scratch.

You came here to look out of the window, not to argue with a satnav. Hand over the keys and the day changes: you arrive relaxed, you stop where the view is good, you have a glass of rosé with lunch, and someone who has driven this coast for over a decade handles the rest.

  • No rental car, no fuel stops, no parking, no scratches on the return inspection.
  • No queue at the taxi rank at 11pm with three suitcases.
  • No app roulette — you know exactly who is collecting you and what it costs.
