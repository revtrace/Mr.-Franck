Some people book Franck once, for the drive in from the airport. Others keep him for the week. Both are normal — and both are priced the same way: a flat figure, agreed in writing, before anyone gets in the car.

Airport & station transfers

Landing is the worst part. Make it the easiest.

You've flown for eight hours. It's 40 degrees on the tarmac, the taxi rank is forty people deep, and the first thing you have to do in a foreign country is negotiate. That's the bit Franck removes.

  • Flight tracked from take-off. Delayed, diverted, early — he adjusts, and it costs you nothing.
  • Met inside arrivals, name on a sign, not "look for a black car in zone C."
  • Bags handled. Bring the golf clubs, the pushchair, the ski bag — just say so when you book.
  • Child seats available on request, at no extra charge.
  • Straight to the door of the hotel, villa or yacht — including the addresses satnavs get wrong.

Nice Côte d'Azur (NCE) is the usual one. Franck also covers Marseille (MRS), Toulon-Hyères (TLN), Genoa (GOA), and the TGV stations at Nice-Ville, Antibes, Cannes and Marseille Saint-Charles.

Cruise port days

You have eight hours and one shot at it. A ship excursion gives you forty strangers and a fixed script. Franck gives you the coast, at your pace, back at the gangway with time to spare.

Villefranche-sur-Mer

The tender port for Nice. Franck meets you at the quay. Èze, Monaco and Monte-Carlo are all inside 30 minutes — the classic day, and comfortably done.

Cannes

Tender in at the Vieux Port. Grasse and the perfume houses, Saint-Paul-de-Vence, Antibes, or simply the Croisette and Mougins for lunch.

Monaco & Marseille

Alongside in Monaco: the Rock, the Casino, and the coast either side. From Marseille: Cassis, the calanques and Aix-en-Provence.

Back on board, guaranteed

Franck plans shore days backwards from your all-aboard time, with a buffer built in for the tender queue. He does this every week in season — he knows how long the ride back from Monaco really takes on a Tuesday in July.

Priced per car, not per head

Four of you costs the same as one. For most couples and families, a private car for the day works out close to — or under — four ship-excursion tickets, and you keep control of the itinerary.

Day trips & excursions

Not a tour. There's no commentary you can't turn off and no gift shop stop. Franck drives, tells you what's worth knowing when you ask, and gets out of the way when you'd rather just look at it.

The classic Riviera day

Èze village on the Moyenne Corniche, Monaco and Monte-Carlo, the Prince's Palace at noon, lunch overlooking the port, back along the coast through Villefranche and Cap Ferrat.

Roughly 7–8 hours

Hilltop villages & perfume

Saint-Paul-de-Vence and the Fondation Maeght, Gourdon on its cliff edge, the Gorges du Loup, and a perfume house in Grasse. Slower, greener, far fewer people.

Roughly 6–8 hours

Provence & the vineyards

West into the Var: Côtes de Provence estates, a long lunch, lavender in season. Franck drives, which is rather the point of a wine day.

Full day · tastings arranged on request

Over the border into Italy

Ventimiglia's Friday market, Bordighera and San Remo, or a longer run to Portofino. Bring a passport or ID card — and an appetite.

Events & the season

The weeks when everything on this coast is full.

Cannes in May. The Grand Prix. The Yacht Show. Weddings in the hills above Mougins. These are the weeks when taxis vanish, apps surge to three times the price and every road into town is closed.

  • Booked well ahead, at a price locked in before the surge.
  • Franck knows the closures, the drop-off points and the back ways in.
  • Late finishes assumed. He waits — you're not hunting for a car at 2am.
  • Multi-day and multi-car arrangements for groups and wedding parties.

Event weeks book out early. If your dates are in May, July or August, message him now rather than closer to the time.
