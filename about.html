About

You're not booking a company. You're booking Franck.

There is no fleet, no call centre and no dispatcher. There is one licensed driver who has worked this coast for over a decade, answers his own phone, and turns up himself — every time.

Franck Auger

Over a decade of these roads.

Ten-plus years of driving the Côte d'Azur teaches you things a map cannot. Which corniche to take at five o'clock. Which restaurant in Èze is worth the climb and which is there for the coach parties. How long the tender queue at Villefranche really is when three ships are in.

That knowledge is most of what you're paying for. The rest is simpler: a clean, comfortable car, a driver who speaks fluent English, and a price agreed before you get in so that nothing about the day is a negotiation.

Most of Franck's guests come from the UK, the United States, Canada and Australia. Many book him once for an airport run, then keep him for the rest of the trip — and message him again the following year before they've even booked flights.

The boring, important part

Anyone can put a photo of a black Mercedes on a website. Here is what actually stands behind the service.

Licensed & registered

A registered French VTC operator (véhicule de tourisme avec chauffeur) — the licensed private-hire category — holding the professional driver's card required to carry paying passengers in France.

Fully insured

Commercial passenger-transport insurance, as French law requires for licensed private hire. Ask and Franck will show you the paperwork — he'd rather you did.

English, properly

Not survival English. Fluent enough to recommend a restaurant, explain what you're looking at, rearrange the afternoon, or sort out a problem at a hotel desk on your behalf.

One car, kept well

A comfortable, air-conditioned saloon, cleaned between every job, with water, phone charging and child seats available on request.

The same driver

Book three journeys in a week and Franck does all three. If he is genuinely unavailable he says so rather than sending a stranger without telling you.

Fixed pricing

One all-in figure per journey, in writing, before you travel — tolls, parking and airport charges included. Nothing added at the end.

How Franck works

Early, not on time

He'd rather wait for you than have you wait for him. For airports, he's there before you've cleared baggage.

Honest about traffic

If you need three hours for Saint-Tropez in August, he'll tell you that, even when you'd rather hear ninety minutes.

Quiet or talkative

Some guests want the history of every village. Some want to sleep. Franck reads the car and adjusts.

Flexible on the day

Want to stay another hour, add a stop, skip the museum for the beach? Say so. Nobody is watching a meter.

Say hello.

Ask a question, sound him out, or send dates. There's no obligation and no deposit — and it's Franck who replies, not a booking system.
